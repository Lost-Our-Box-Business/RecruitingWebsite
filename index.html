What We Do

For Companies

Share your open role and we go source, screen for fit, and bring you a shortlist of candidates who are ready to talk — no cost unless you hire.

For Candidates

We work with engineers and product people actively exploring new roles, matching you to companies and teams that fit — never a blind spam list.

Our Focus

Software engineering, infrastructure, and product roles. We know the difference between a backend req and a platform req, and we recruit accordingly.

How It Works

  1. Tell us the role. Title, stack, seniority, and what "good" looks like for the team.
  2. We source and screen. Candidates are organized and qualified against your criteria — we don't rank or score people, we present who fits.
  3. You interview and decide. We stay in the loop to keep things moving, but the hiring call is always yours.
  4. Contingency fee, only on a hire. No placement, no fee.
